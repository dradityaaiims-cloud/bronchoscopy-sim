Tips

  • At a carina, flex the tip and roll the shaft until a lumen is centred, then advance. Rolling with a flexed tip sweeps the aim around.
  • The 3D compass (bottom right of the scope view) shows the patient's axes as seen from the scope: Anterior/Posterior, Right/Left, Superior/Inferior. Letters nearer to you are brighter. At roll 0 anterior is at 12 o'clock and the right lung is on the right of the screen.
  • The articulation gauge plots the tip bend (distance from centre) and the direction it points around the airway (angle).
  • Drag in the right panel to rotate the 3D lungs; scroll to zoom.
